A Letter For You 💌

My Soon-to-Be Wife, ❤️

I never thought I would sit down and write a handwritten letter, but then again, I also never thought our story would happen this way.

Sometimes I genuinely feel that Shyam Baba writes stories in the most unexpected ways.

There are many things I admire about you, but what touches my heart the most is the kind of person you are. The way you remember small things, the way you care, the way you adjust so naturally, and most importantly, the way you value family.

And I want to tell you something honestly — talking to you feels peaceful. In a world where everything feels rushed and noisy, you somehow feel calm. Maybe that is why I secretly like calling you my peace.

"Prasad mein dosh nahi dekha jaata, usse shraddha aur prem se grahan kiya jaata hai."

So maybe neither of us will be perfect — because we are human after all — but I want to try my best to focus more on the person than the flaws. To understand more, judge less, and remind myself that something given by Baba deserves gratitude first.

Also, I have one serious complaint. I still don't understand how someone's cheeks can look so much like rasmalai 😄 And before you say anything — yes, I still stand by my statement. 😂

We are still at the beginning of our story, still learning each other, still slowly falling in love — and I think that is the beautiful part. No rushing, no drama, just two people slowly becoming each other's comfort.

I don't know what all is written ahead for us, but I do know one thing — I feel grateful. Grateful that life gave us another chance to reconnect, grateful for this unexpected journey, and grateful for you.

Maybe this was always Shyam Baba's plan. 🙏

Yours (officially and spiritually approved by Shyam Baba 😌),
Mukul
